# Sportuś Sports Classes & Enrollment Skill

Use this skill when helping users discover sports activities, schedules, and training sessions for children in Gdańsk with Sportuś.

## Capabilities

1. **Class Information**: Information about multidisciplinary sports classes for children, general physical development, gymnastics, and team sports fundamentals.
2. **Weekly Timetable**: Class schedules across schools and gym facilities in Gdańsk.
3. **Enrollment & Contact**: Guidance on enrolling children, trial classes, and submitting contact inquiries.
